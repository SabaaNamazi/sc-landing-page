The Student's Journey

PHASE 01

Face In, You're In

Existing students can log in instantly using Face ID. The system recognizes their registered face and securely takes them to their student dashboard.

PHASE 02

Quick Registration

New students can quickly register by providing their name, face, and voice. The system captures their face and voice embeddings and creates their profile for future attendance recognition.

PHASE 03

Scan, Join & Go

Students can join a class instantly through the shared QR code or course link and get automatically enrolled in the selected subject.

PHASE 04

Student's Dashboard

Students can view all their enrolled subjects in one place, along with course details.
